The rules go to Congress

Some players call a rule a bug. Others call it the game. OpenCol doesn't pick a side: a change like that becomes a rule option. At 0 it is the original's rule, and its default is argued out in public, on the issue and on Discord, before it is merged.

A game keeps the rules it started with, in its saved games too. A 1994 saved game plays by the original's rules.

How a rule is decided

The same four steps for every one. When it is not clear what kind of change something is, ask in the issue before writing the code.

  1. An issue says what changes, and proposes a default and why.
  2. Discussion on the issue and on Discord: which variant, what the computer players do, the default.
  3. The maintainers decide, and write the decision on the issue.
  4. The change is made with a rules test. With every option at 0, OpenCol still plays as the 1995 game.
