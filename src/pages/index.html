The rules go to Congress

Some players call a rule a bug. Others call it the game. OpenCol doesn't pick a side: a change like that becomes a rule option. At 0 it is the original's rule, and its default gets argued out in public, on the issue and on Discord, before it is merged.

Two of them are on in 0.10. Two more are proposed. The floor is open.

Join the Discord Propose a rule Report a bug

Something plays differently from the 1995 game? Attach a save from just before it, and OPENCOL.LOG. Want to help build it? Your first change takes about 30 minutes.

Three steps, no DOSBox

  1. Have the 1994 game. OpenCol contains none of it. The Classic on Steam or GOG, not Civilization IV: Colonization.
  2. Unzip OpenCol anywhere you can write: not Program Files. On a Mac, the first time, right-click opencol and choose Open.
  3. Start it, and show it the folder with VICEROY.EXE. It remembers. To upgrade, unzip the next version over the old one.
